## 1. Decision Title

**Adopt a single CRM platform as the enterprise customer master, serving both
wholesale (B2B) and retail/loyalty (B2C), rolled out wholesale-first.**

**ADR Status**: Proposed (pending evaluation outcome and COO/CEO sign-off)

## 2. Stakeholders

- **Deciders (A):** Wilma Flintstone (COO), Fred Flintstone (CEO — investment)
- **Consulted (C):** Barney Rubble (Wholesale), Betty Rubble (Retail), George Jetson
  (Digital), Velma Dinkley (advisor), Project 003/004/005 leads
- **Informed (I):** Store managers, Order & Supply Chain team

## 3. Context

Spoke & Rim has no CRM. Two credible architectures exist:

- **Option A — Single platform:** one CRM is the customer master for trade
  accounts, retail customers, and loyalty. B2B and B2C are data partitions
  and role-based views, not separate systems.
- **Option B — Dual-stack:** a B2B sales CRM for wholesale plus a retail
  marketing/loyalty platform (e.g. Klaviyo-class) for B2C, with sync.

The portfolio roadmap (ARC-000-PORT §2.3) requires exactly one customer
identity source for Projects 004 (POS loyalty) and 005 (e-commerce accounts).

## 4. Decision

**Option A — single platform.** The evaluation (per ARC-002-RSCH shortlist:
HubSpot, Dynamics 365, Zoho) selects one product; that product's customer
object model becomes the enterprise customer master. Rollout is phased:
wholesale accounts first (Q1–Q2 FY27), then loyalty/retail (Q2–Q3 FY27),
aligned to the POS pilot in Project 004.

## 5. Rationale

1. **One identity, one consent record.** Dual-stack splits consent and
   identity across systems — the exact Privacy Act / Spam Act exposure this
   project exists to remove (SD-3, G-3, G-4).
2. **Portfolio dependency.** Projects 004 and 005 integrate once, against one
   API (G-6, INT-002/003). Dual-stack doubles their integration surface.
3. **Cross-channel value.** A trade-account owner who shops retail is one
   record — the single-view driver (SD-4) is unachievable in Option B without
   building the very MDM layer we are trying to buy.
4. **TCO.** One licence + one implementation partner fits BR-008; dual-stack
   estimates exceed it once sync build/maintenance is priced.

## 6. Consequences

- **Positive:** single consent audit trail; one integration point for the
  portfolio; simpler operating model for 160 staff.
- **Negative / accepted:** the chosen suite's loyalty module may be weaker
  than a best-of-breed retail platform — accepted, with the revisit trigger
  below; wholesale and retail teams share one platform roadmap and must
  co-govern configuration changes.
- **Neutral:** Mailchimp is decommissioned after re-permission (DAT-001).

## 7. Revisit Trigger

If evaluation scoring shows **no shortlisted suite reaches ≥ 70% on the
loyalty/marketing criteria group**, Option B is re-opened with the CRM
designated master and the retail platform as a governed subscriber — decided
by a superseding ADR, not by drift.
