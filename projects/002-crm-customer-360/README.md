## Summary

Establish a single customer master across ~250 wholesale trade accounts and
~38,000 retail/loyalty members, replacing spreadsheets and a disconnected
Mailchimp list. Delivers auditable marketing consent (Privacy Act / Spam Act),
an in-platform loyalty engine, and the customer-master API on which Projects
004 (POS) and 005 (E-Commerce) depend.
