## 1. Decision Title

**A cloud ERP will replace MYOB AccountRight as the financial system of
record, with cutover sequenced after Projects 004 and 005 go-live. This ADR
defines the supersession path for ARC-001-ADR-001, which remains in force
until cutover.**

**ADR Status**: Proposed

## 2. Stakeholders

- **Decider (A):** Nathan Dyke (CEO)
- **Consulted (C):** Grace Liu (Finance — sponsor), Kirralee Dyke (COO),
  Chris McKelt (advisor), external accountant, Projects 001/002/004/005 leads
- **Informed (I):** All staff via change programme

## 3. Context

ARC-001-ADR-001 (June 2026) retained MYOB AccountRight to de-risk the
inventory uplift — correctly, at that time. The scenario's growth (two
entities, 12 stores, 160 staff, multi-state payroll, Peppol demand, 12-day
close) has moved MYOB from "trusted system of record" to "constraint"
(ARC-003-STKE SD-1..SD-4). Meanwhile, the hub-first integration pattern
adopted across the portfolio means the accounting platform now has exactly
**one** upstream integration: the hub.

## 4. Decision

1. A cloud ERP (selected via ARC-003-RSCH shortlist and formal evaluation)
   becomes the financial system of record.
2. **Timing gate:** implementation may not begin before Projects 004 and 005
   are live (ARC-000-PORT PR-5). Target: implementation FY27 Q4, cutover FY28.
3. **Supersession mechanics:** at cutover, ARC-001-ADR-001 status changes to
   `SUPERSEDED by ARC-003-ADR-001` with the cutover date recorded; MYOB is
   retained read-only for 7 years (BR-010).
4. **Integration invariant:** the only integration change at cutover is
   re-pointing hub→MYOB to hub→ERP (G-5). Any design requiring changes in
   Projects 002/004/005 fails design review.

## 5. Options Considered

| Option | Summary | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A — Stay on AccountRight + point solutions | Payroll, consolidation, Peppol bolted on around MYOB | Rejected: multiplies integrations, leaves close/landed-cost pain (SD-1, SD-3) |
| B — Upgrade within family (MYOB Acumatica) presumptively | Skip open evaluation | Rejected as *presumption*; carried as shortlist option #1 on merit |
| **C — Open evaluation, cloud ERP, gated timing (chosen)** | Compete Acumatica / Business Central / Odoo; cutover last | **Accepted** — best capability outcome and contained risk |
| D — ERP now, in parallel with 004/005 | Fastest finance relief | Rejected: violates PR-5; highest portfolio destabilisation risk (SD-5) |

## 6. Rationale

1. The hub-first pattern converts an ERP migration from a portfolio-wide
   re-integration into a single re-pointed interface — the cheapest moment in
   the company's history to change accounting platforms is *after* the hub is
   the only thing talking to it.
2. Finance compliance drivers (STP Phase 2, Peppol, consolidation) are
   structural, not configurational — Option A defers rather than resolves.
3. Governed supersession preserves decision traceability: ADR-001 was right
   then; this ADR records why the context changed, not that it was wrong.

## 7. Sub-Decision: Payroll Pattern

Best-of-breed AU payroll (award interpretation, STP2-certified) paired with
the ERP is the **default pattern** entering evaluation, per ARC-003-RSCH R-1.
An ERP-native payroll may displace it only by scoring ≥ 85% on the payroll
criteria group including reference-checked award handling.

## 8. Consequences

- **Positive:** contained cutover; finance goals G-1..G-6 unblocked; portfolio
  decision record demonstrates ArcKit supersession discipline end-to-end.
- **Negative / accepted:** finance carries MYOB pain ~18 more months; dual
  familiarity (MYOB + ERP) needed during transition; TCO envelope BR-009 must
  hold through evaluation.
- **Conditions:** DAT-002 reconciliation evidence contractualised;
  parallel pay runs ≥ 2 cycles; `/arckit-au:pia` for employee data migration.
